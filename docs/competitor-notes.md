### Takeaways

- **Night / dark / moonlit ocean is a live sub-niche.** It matches Ocean Night exactly, and small
  channels (1–5K subscribers) are breaking out with it.
- **Lengths.** Music with waves performs at 1 h and 3 h. Sounds-only and black-screen videos
  run 8–12 h. Start with 3 h, the proven length that fits our 15-minute songs (12 songs).
- **Black or dark screen** is a recurring format: people sleep with the screen on. Offer a
  dark-screen edition alongside a slow night-ocean visual.
- **"No loop break"** is a selling point people search for. Our music never loops (every
  minute is newly composed), so that's an honest differentiator for titles and descriptions.
- **Theme variations that do well:** moonlit, cosmic or bioluminescent ocean, coastal night
  with fireplace, ocean with gentle piano.
